**Ethnolinguistic Naming Conventions for the World of Aethelgard (Revised)**

| **Faction / Region** | **Linguistic Inspiration (Real-World Analog)** | **Phonetic Feel / Naming Convention** | **Example Names (Character & Place)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Sunken Coast (Coastal Trade Cities)** | Basque / Polynesian | Fluid vowels, double consonants, 'k' and 'z' sounds. Names often reflect the sea, trade, or vibrant life. | *Characters:* Kaia, Zorion, Lani, Kaiho. *Places:* Azkuna, Olasore, Kaikuta, Zubiaga Port. |
| **The Veiled Highlands (Mystic Seers & Hermits)** | Gaelic / Estonian | Soft, ethereal sounds, often with 'ae', 'ei', 'y' vowels. Names evoke ancient wisdom, mist, or quietude. | *Characters:* Aeliana, Kaelen, Lyra, Eirwyn. *Places:* Mystwood, Lyrebright, Aethelhym, Shadowfen Reach. |
| **The Ashforged Clans (Volcanic/Industrial Forges)** | Georgian / Slavic | Robust, guttural, clusters of consonants, 'kh', 'dz', 'ts'. Names relate to stone, fire, and endurance. | *Characters:* Grigor, Zviad, Khatuna, Tamar. *Places:* Khazad-Dum (No, just kidding! It's too on the nose!), Tsakhra Peak, Dzveli Forge, Goris Maw. |
| **The Riverbend Concord (Agricultural Communes)** | Old French / Low German | Gentle, pastoral, often single or two syllables. Names suggest peace, growth, or community. | *Characters:* Anneliese, Theron, Maeva, Elara. *Places:* Meadowbrook, River's Bend, Greenvale, Hearthstone Acre. |
| **The Shifting Sands (Nomadic Sky-Sailors)** | Tuareg / Mongolian | Flowing yet sharp, often with 'z', 'gh', 'kh' sounds. Names are often tied to sky, wind, or open spaces. | *Characters:* Zayid, Ghazan, Aziza, Khalida. *Places:* Zephyr Oasis, The Sky-Sands, Ghazali Pass, The Whispering Dune. |
